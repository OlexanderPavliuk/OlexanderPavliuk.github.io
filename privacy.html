Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026.

This OAuth client is used only by its owner with rclone for personal backups.

  • It requests only the drive.file scope: access to files it created itself in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot see any other files.
  • It uploads, lists and deletes only its own encrypted backup files.
  • No data is collected from, shared with or sold to anyone. There are no other users, analytics or advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
